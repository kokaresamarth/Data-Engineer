Interview perspective
=====================

1) WHAT ARE YOU DOING?
----------------------
-> Reading a data from AWS S3 Bucket.
-> Data generated by transactional system.
-> Transforming the data based on business use case.
-> writing the data into data mart.

2) WHY ARE WE DOING?
--------------------
-> Business units wants to incentivies the billing persion 
   based on total sales?
-> Customer spending is being tracked and based on discount    is provided.
-> Daily report needs to be generated.
-> Monthly report also needs to be generated at end of    every month

3) HOW ARE WE DOING?
--------------------
-> Spark has been used for transformation.
-> Data volume was 15 GB per day.
-> S3 boto SDK has been used to connect with S3.
-> Data pushed to data mart for reporting to further    process.
